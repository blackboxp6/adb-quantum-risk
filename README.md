# ADB Quantum Risk

A quantum computing approach to catastrophe risk modeling for regional disaster risk financing.

This project explores the use of quantum computing to represent and simulate correlated catastrophe risks across multiple countries, with applications to regional risk pooling and disaster financing.

## Objective

Investigate whether quantum state preparation and structured representations of risk can offer a scalable alternative to explicit joint-state enumeration in regional catastrophe modeling.

## Research Direction

- Quantum representation of regional catastrophe risk
- Modeling dependencies through shared risk factors
- Structured state preparation and scalability
- Applications to disaster risk financing and regional risk pooling

## Status

Research prototype developed for the **ADB Quantum Innovation Challenge 2026**.
